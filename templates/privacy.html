Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register using our platform or via third-party services (like Google or Facebook), we collect your Name, Email Address, and Phone Number. We also store the digital files (designs, documents, photos) you explicitly upload for printing.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to create your account, process your printing orders, provide order status updates via email, and facilitate secure delivery or pickup.

3. Data Protection

We implement strict security measures to protect your personal data. Passwords are securely hashed, and uploaded files are stored safely via our cloud providers.

4. Data Deletion Instructions

You have the right to request the deletion of your data at any time. You can instantly delete your account, your order history, and all associated files by navigating to your Manage Account (Profile) dashboard and clicking the "Delete My Account" button. Alternatively, you can contact our support team to request manual deletion.
